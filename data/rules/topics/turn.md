## Phases

- A turn has three phases, in this order: Action, Buy, Clean-up. [official]
- Nocturne adds a Night phase between Buy and Clean-up, for playing Night cards. In that phase you can play any number of Night cards. [official]
- Some things happen "at the start of your turn", before your Action phase. Examples are the next-turn effects of {rule:durations|Durations}. If several happen at once, you choose the order.

## Action phase

- You start the turn with 1 Action. Playing an Action card uses up an Action. [official]
- Cards that give **+Actions** let you play more Action cards this turn.
- You don't have to play any Action cards.
- Do what a card says from top to bottom, and as much of it as you can. If a card asks for something impossible, skip that part and carry on. [official]

## Buy phase

- Play Treasures from your hand, in any order, then buy cards. You don't have to play every Treasure.
- Once you've bought a card, you can't play any more Treasures this turn.
- You start the turn with 1 Buy. Each Buy lets you buy one card (or Event or Project) whose cost you can pay. [official]
- Bought cards come from the Supply and go to your discard pile. [official]
- Unspent coins, Actions and Buys are lost at the end of the turn.

## Clean-up

- Discard every card in play and every card left in your hand. Cards you set aside on purpose, and {rule:durations|Durations} with work left to do on a later turn, stay where they are. [official]
- Then draw a new hand of 5 cards, shuffling if you need to. [official]
- The next player to the left takes their turn. [official]

## Drawing and shuffling

- If you need to draw, reveal or look at a card from your deck and your deck is empty, shuffle your discard pile to make a new deck. [official]
  - Shuffle only when you actually need a card and the deck has run out, never in advance.
  - If your deck and discard pile are both empty, you simply don't get the card.
- Cards set aside or revealed during an effect aren't in your discard pile, so they aren't shuffled in.

## Buying versus gaining

- Buying a card is one way of gaining it. Effects that say "gain" give you a card without spending coins or a Buy.
- "When you gain" abilities trigger whether or not you bought the card. "When you buy" abilities trigger only on a buy.
- Gained cards go to your discard pile unless the effect says otherwise (to your hand, onto your deck ...). [official]
- You can only gain a card that's actually there. If its pile is empty (or the card is covered in a {rule:split-piles|split pile}), you gain nothing.
