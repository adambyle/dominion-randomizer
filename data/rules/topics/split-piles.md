A split pile is a single Kingdom pile made of differently named cards in a set order. It counts as one of the 10 Kingdom piles.

## Kinds of split pile

- **Empires:** five piles with 5 copies each of two cards, cheaper card on top: {card:Catapult/Rocks}, {card:Encampment/Plunder}, {card:Gladiator/Fortune}, {card:Patrician/Emporium} and {card:Settlers/Bustling Village}. The promo {card:Sauna/Avanto} works the same way.
- **Allies:** six piles with 4 copies each of four cards, in cost order: {card:Augurs}, {card:Clashes}, {card:Forts}, {card:Odysseys}, {card:Townsfolk} and {card:Wizards}. Their top cards can **rotate** the pile.
- **{card:Castles}:** eight different Castles in cost order. With 2 players use one of each, 8 cards in all; with more players use all 12.
- {card:Knights} and Ruins also mix different cards in one pile, but they are shuffled face down instead of being in a set order.

## Buying and gaining

- Only the top card of a split pile can be bought or gained. [official]
- You can look through a split pile at any time, as long as you don't change its order. [official]
- In Empires piles, tip the bottom five cards sideways so everyone remembers they're there.
- A card returned to its pile goes on top, even if that covers cards that were showing. Examples are {card:Encampment} returning itself and {card:Ambassador} returning a card. [official]

## Rotating

- To rotate a pile, take the top card and every copy of it directly underneath, and put them on the bottom of the pile. [official]
- For example, with 3 {card:Herb Gatherer|Herb Gatherers} left on top of the Augurs, rotating moves all 3 to the bottom and leaves {card:Acolyte} on top.
- Rotating is optional whenever a card says "you may".

## The pile as a whole

- When an effect cares about the cost or types *of a pile*, use what the randomizer card says. That is usually the original top card. [official]
  - So {card:Gladiator/Fortune} counts as a {cost:3} Action pile. It can be {card:Young Witch}'s Bane even though {card:Fortune} costs more and isn't an Action.
- Anything that affects a pile affects every card from it. [official]
  - For example, a {card:Training} token on the Odysseys gives {coin:+1} when you play {card:Sunken Treasure}, even though it's a Treasure.
- The pile is empty only when every card in it is gone. This matters for the game end and for cards that count empty piles. [official]
