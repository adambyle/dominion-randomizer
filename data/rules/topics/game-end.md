## When the game ends

- The game ends at the end of a turn if the {card:Province} pile is empty. [official]
- It also ends if any 3 Supply piles are empty, or any 4 with 5 or 6 players. [official]
- With Prosperity's {card:Colony} in the game, it also ends if the Colony pile is empty. [official]
- These conditions are checked only at the end of a turn. A pile emptied and refilled within the same turn doesn't count. The current turn always finishes first. Once the game is over, extra turns that haven't started yet (from {card:Outpost}, for example) don't happen.

## Which piles count

- Every Supply pile counts, including {card:Curse}, {card:Copper} and the other basic piles.
- Piles outside the Supply don't count. Examples are {card:Spoils}, {card:Horse|Horses}, Prizes and Rewards, and piles set aside for a card such as {card:Ferryman}.
- A {rule:split-piles|split pile} is empty only once every card in it is gone. Emptying just the top half doesn't count.

## Scoring

- Count the victory points (VP) in every card you own: your deck, hand and discard pile, plus cards on mats or set aside. [official]
- {card:Curse|Curses} are worth {vp:-1} each. Add any VP tokens and points from Landmarks.
- The highest score wins. If tied, the tied player who had fewer turns wins. If they had the same number of turns, they share the win. [official]
