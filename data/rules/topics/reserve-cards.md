## How Reserves work

- When you play a Reserve card, you put it on your Tavern mat. It isn't in play there, so it isn't discarded in Clean-up. [official]
- Each Reserve says when it can be called. To call it, move it from your mat into play and do what it says. It's then discarded in that turn's Clean-up. [official]
- Calling is optional, and calling one Reserve doesn't use up an Action.
- You can call it on another player's turn if its timing allows; it's then discarded in that turn's Clean-up.
- At the end of the game, cards on your Tavern mat count as part of your deck. [official]

## Edge cases

- If something stops a Reserve from moving itself to the mat (for example, playing it with {card:Band of Misfits}), it stays where it is. See {rule:stop-moving-rule}.
- Calling it puts it in play but doesn't count as playing it.
