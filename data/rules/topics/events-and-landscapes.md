Landscapes are the sideways cards set out beside the Kingdom. They aren't Kingdom cards and don't count toward the 10 Kingdom piles.

## Using them

- The usual recommendation is no more than two landscapes per game, in any mix. Some sets suggest at most one of certain kinds (one Ally, one Prophecy). [official]
- Landscapes are never in anyone's deck and can't be gained, trashed or played.

## Events (Adventures, Empires, Menagerie, Plunder)

- You buy an Event in your Buy phase with a Buy and its cost, like a card, but you don't gain anything; its ability happens instead. [official]
- Buying an Event isn't buying a card, so "when you buy a card" effects don't trigger. [official]
- Some Events say "once per turn" or "once per game".

## Projects (Renaissance)

- Buy a Project with a Buy to put one of your cubes on it. From then on its ability works for you for the rest of the game. Each player can buy each Project only once. [official]

## Landmarks (Empires)

- Landmarks aren't bought. They change scoring or the rules for everyone all game. [official]

## Ways (Menagerie)

- Each time you play an Action card, you may use a Way's text instead of the card's own instructions. [official]
- Cards that would move themselves when played (like {card:Horse}) stay in play if you use a Way instead.

## Traits (Plunder)

- Each Trait goes on one Action or Treasure Kingdom pile, chosen at random during setup. Every card from that pile has the Trait's ability in addition to its own. [official]

## Allies (Allies) and Prophecies (Rising Sun)

- An Ally is used when a Liaison is in the game. Each player starts with 1 Favor, and Liaisons give more; spend Favors on the Ally's ability. [official]
- A Prophecy is used when an Omen is in the game. It starts with Sun tokens on it; Omens remove them, and once the last one is gone the Prophecy is active for the rest of the game. [official]
