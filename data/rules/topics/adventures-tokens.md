Each player has their own set of these tokens, taken only when a card puts them into play.

## Tokens on piles

- **+1 Card, +1 Action, +1 Buy, +{coin:1}:** when you play a card from the pile with your token, you first get that bonus. [official]
- **–{cost:2} cost:** cards from that pile cost {cost:2} less on your turns. [official]
- **Trashing:** when you buy a card from that pile, you may trash a card from your hand. [official]
- **Estate:** goes on the card set aside by {card:Inheritance}.
- A token goes on an Action Supply pile, judged by the pile's randomizer for {rule:split-piles|split piles}. Moving it to a new pile takes it off the old one.

## Tokens that wait for something

- **–1 Card:** the next time you would draw cards, draw one fewer and remove the token. [official]
- **–{coin:1}:** the next time you would get coins, get {coin:1} less and remove the token. [official]
- **Journey:** starts face up and is turned over by some cards. What happens depends on which way it ends up.
