## Attacks

- An Attack affects other players in turn order, starting with the player to your left.
- Do the rest of the card as normal. Players who are unaffected by the Attack (because of a Reaction, {card:Lighthouse} and so on) skip only the parts that affect them.
- Players do as much of an Attack as they can. If you're told to discard down to 3 cards and you already have 3 or fewer, nothing happens.

## Reactions

- A Reaction says when it can be used: for example "when another player plays an Attack card" ({card:Moat}) or "when you gain a card".
- Reacting is optional. Revealing a Reaction from your hand usually keeps it in your hand.
- Reactions to an Attack happen after the Attack is played but before it does anything. [official]
- If several of your Reactions trigger at once, you choose the order.
