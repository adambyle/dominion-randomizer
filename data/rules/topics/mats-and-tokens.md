## Coffers (Guilds, Renaissance)

- "+1 Coffers" means put a coin token on your Coffers mat. [official]
- On your turn you can spend Coffers for {coin:1} each, usually in your Buy phase before buying. Each token is used up when spent.

## Villagers (Renaissance)

- "+1 Villager" means put a token on your Villagers mat. [official]
- In your Action phase you can spend Villagers for +1 Action each. Each token is used up when spent.

## Favors (Allies)

- Liaisons give Favor tokens. Each player starts the game with 1 Favor when an Ally is in use. Spend Favors on the Ally's ability. [official]

## VP tokens (Prosperity, Empires)

- {vp:1} tokens are worth 1 VP each at the end of the game. Keep yours in front of you; they aren't cards.
- Some cards and Landmarks put VP tokens on piles or on themselves to be taken later.

## Artifacts (Renaissance)

- An Artifact belongs to one player at a time. Taking it, even from another player, means it's now yours and works for you. [official]
- There's only one of each, so taking it when someone else has it takes it away from them.
