## Cost changes

- Effects like {card:Bridge} or {card:Highway} lower costs everywhere (in the Supply, in hands, in decks) for the rest of the turn, never below {cost:0}. [official]
- Anything that checks a cost uses the current cost, so a cheaper card can now be gained with a "costing up to" effect.
- Coins you have never raise a "costing up to" limit. [official]

## Potions (Alchemy)

- {potion} in a cost must be paid with {potion}, which comes from the {card:Potion} Treasure. [official]
- Effects that gain "a card costing up to {cost:X}" can't gain a card with {potion} in its cost, unless they say otherwise.

## Debt (Empires)

- Some costs include {debt:}. You can buy such a card without coins for that part; instead you take that many Debt tokens. [official]
- While you have Debt, you can't buy cards, Events or Projects. [official]
- You can pay off Debt with coins, {coin:1} per token, at any time during your turn (the earlier rule allowed it in your Buy phase and Clean-up). [official]
- Effects that gain "a card costing up to {cost:X}" can't gain a card with {debt:} in its cost.

## Comparing mixed costs

- One cost is more than another only if it's more in at least one of coins, {potion} or {debt:}, and not less in any. [official]
- So {cost:4} and {debt:4} aren't more or less than each other; neither is "cheaper".
- Costs are only equal if every part matches.
