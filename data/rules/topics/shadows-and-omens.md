## Shadow cards

- When you shuffle, put any Shadow cards being shuffled on the bottom of the new deck, under the other cards. [official]
- Whenever you could play an Action card from your hand, you may play a Shadow card from your deck instead, as if it were in your hand. [official]
- This includes being told to play an Action from your hand by another card. A Shadow played from your deck still uses up an Action as normal.
- Only playing is allowed; other effects that look at your hand don't see Shadows in your deck.

## Omens and the Prophecy

- In a game with an Omen, use one Prophecy. Put Sun tokens on it: 5 with 2 players, 8 with 3, 10 with 4, 12 with 5 and 13 with 6. [official]
- "{sun:+}" on an Omen means remove a Sun token from the Prophecy. [official]
- When the last Sun token is removed, the Prophecy takes effect for everyone for the rest of the game, starting immediately. [official]
- Some Prophecies change setup (like {card:Approaching Army}); that setup happens at the start of the game anyway.
