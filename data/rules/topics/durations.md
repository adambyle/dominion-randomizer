## Staying in play

- Duration cards are orange and do things on later turns, usually at the start of your next turn. [official]
- A Duration stays in play until the Clean-up of the last turn on which it does something. Then it's discarded as normal. [official]
- If a Duration has nothing left to do (for example, its effect couldn't happen), it's discarded in this turn's Clean-up like any other card.
- If a Duration leaves play early, it stops doing things on future turns. [official]
- Durations left over from an earlier turn are in play, so they count for effects that look at cards you have in play. They weren't played this turn, though.

## Playing a Duration more than once

- If you play a Duration twice with {card:Throne Room} or a similar card, you get its later effect twice too.
- The card that replayed it stays in play for as long as the Duration does, as a reminder. [official]

## Start-of-turn effects

- Next-turn effects happen at the start of your turn, before your Action phase.
- If several happen at once, you choose the order.
- "Your next turn" means your very next one, even if that's an extra turn (from {card:Outpost}, for example).
