The Setup panel on the table lists everything a dealt kingdom needs, including the extra piles, mats and tokens that individual cards ask for. This page covers the parts that are the same in every game.

## The Supply

- Choose 10 Kingdom piles. Each one is 10 copies of the card. [official]
  - A Kingdom pile of Victory cards uses 8 copies with 2 players and 12 with 3 or more. [official]
  - A split pile counts as one of the 10. See {rule:split-piles}.
- Every game also uses the basic piles: {card:Copper}, {card:Silver}, {card:Gold}, {card:Estate}, {card:Duchy}, {card:Province} and {card:Curse}. [official]
- {card:Estate} and {card:Duchy}: 8 each with 2 players, 12 each with 3 or more. [official]
- {card:Province}: 8 with 2 players, 12 with 3–4, 15 with 5 and 18 with 6. [official]
- {card:Curse}: 10 for each player after the first, so 10 with 2 players up to 50 with 6. [official]
- {card:Silver} and {card:Gold}: the whole pile (40 and 30).
- {card:Copper}: whatever is left after the starting decks are dealt.
- With 5 or 6 players a single set doesn't have enough basic cards. Add the basic cards from a second base set (or the Base Cards box). This app assumes 120 Coppers in that case.

## Platinum, Colony and Shelters

- {card:Platinum} and {card:Colony} (Prosperity) and Shelters (Dark Ages) aren't used every game.
- The rulebooks suggest deciding at random, in proportion to how many of the Kingdom cards come from that set. For example, use them if a randomly chosen Kingdom card comes from that set. [official]
- Unless you pick yourself in Tune, this app uses Platinum and Colony when the first card dealt is from Prosperity, and Shelters when the last card dealt is from Dark Ages.
- With Shelters, each player starts with {card:Hovel}, {card:Necropolis} and {card:Overgrown Estate} instead of 3 Estates. The Estate pile is still in the Supply.

## Starting decks

- Each player starts with 7 {card:Copper|Coppers} and 3 {card:Estate|Estates}. [official]
  - The Coppers come out of the Copper pile. The starting Estates are extra cards, so the Estate pile still has its full 8 or 12.
  - Heirlooms (Nocturne) replace starting Coppers, one for each card that has one.
- Shuffle your 10 cards to form your deck, then draw 5 to make your first hand. [official]

## Before the first turn

- Leave room for the trash. One trash pile is shared by everyone. [official]
- Apply each card's own setup instructions (text below a line, "Setup:" text, Heirlooms, tokens and so on). These apply to every card used in the game, including ones outside the Supply.
- Choose the first player at random. Play then goes clockwise. [official]
