The lose-track rule came in with Dark Ages and was replaced by the {rule:stop-moving-rule} in the 2019 errata. Use the stop-moving rule today. This page is here because older FAQs and rulebooks talk about "losing track" of cards.

## What it said

- If an effect tries to move a card that isn't where the effect expects it, the card doesn't move. The effect has "lost track" of it. [official]
- Losing track of a card stops only the movement. Everything else in the effect still happens. [official]
- An effect doesn't lose track of a card it moved itself, only of cards that something else moved. [official]

## What changed in 2019

- Under the old rule, a card covered up on top of a discard pile was lost track of. Now a card in a discard pile can still be moved after it's covered.
- The new rule also states outright that an effect can always move a card when it says where the card comes from.
