Adventures has two lines of Travellers: {card:Page} → {card:Treasure Hunter} → {card:Warrior} → {card:Hero} → {card:Champion}, and {card:Peasant} → {card:Soldier} → {card:Fugitive} → {card:Disciple} → {card:Teacher}.

## Exchanging

- When you discard a Traveller from play, you may exchange it for the next card in its line: return it to its pile and take the next one, to your discard pile. [official]
- Exchanging isn't gaining or trashing, so "when you gain" or "when you trash" effects don't happen. [official]
- If the next pile is empty, you can't exchange. [official]
- Only the Page and Peasant piles are in the Supply. The other Traveller piles aren't, and they don't count for pile endings.
- If the Traveller has already been moved elsewhere (for example put on your deck by {card:Scheme}), it can't be exchanged. See {rule:stop-moving-rule}.
