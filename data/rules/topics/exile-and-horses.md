## Exile

- To Exile a card, put it on your Exile mat. Cards there aren't in play or in your deck, but they're still yours and count for scoring. [official]
- When you gain a card, you may discard all copies of it from your Exile mat. [official]
- That's the only usual way to get cards back out of Exile.

## Horses

- Horses come from their own pile outside the Supply, so "a card costing up to" effects can't gain them; only cards that say "gain a Horse" can. [official]
- When you play a Horse, it returns to its pile. [official]
- The Horse pile doesn't count for game end. See {rule:game-end}.
