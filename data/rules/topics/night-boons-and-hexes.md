## Night

- After your Buy phase comes your Night phase, where you may play any number of Night cards. Night cards aren't Actions and don't use up Actions. [official]
- Night is after buying, so a card gained to your hand in your Buy phase (like {card:Ghost Town}) can usually be played the same turn.

## Boons

- Fate cards make you receive Boons. To receive one, turn over the top Boon, do what it says, then discard it, unless it says to keep it until Clean-up. [official]
- When the Boons deck runs out, shuffle its discards to form a new one. [official]
- Some Boons give out a Spirit, such as {card:Will-o'-Wisp}.

## Hexes

- Doom cards make players receive Hexes. A Hex works the same way as a Boon but is bad for whoever receives it. [official]
- When a Doom Attack makes "each other player" receive a Hex, they all receive the same Hex. [official]
- Two Hexes give States: {card:Deluded} or {card:Envious}, and {card:Miserable}.

## States

- A State sits in front of the player who has it and changes their game until it says to return it. [official]
- {card:Lost in the Woods} (from {card:Fool}) and the Hex-given States work this way.

## Heirlooms

- Some Kingdom cards come with an Heirloom. In games using that card, each player replaces one of their starting {card:Copper|Coppers} with it. [official]
- Heirlooms aren't in the Supply and have no pile of their own.

## Spirits and other non-Supply cards

- {card:Will-o'-Wisp}, {card:Imp} and {card:Ghost} are Spirits, with their own piles outside the Supply. {card:Bat}, {card:Wish} and the {card:Zombie Apprentice|Zombies} are also outside the Supply.
- Non-Supply piles don't count for game end. See {rule:game-end}.
